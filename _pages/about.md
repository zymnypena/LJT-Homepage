## About Me

I am Junteng Liu (刘俊腾), a first-year PhD candidate at the [HKUST NLP Group](https://hkust-nlp.github.io/), Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, supervised by [Professor Junxian He](https://jxhe.github.io/). I graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024, where I was also previously advised by Professor Junxian He during my undergraduate studies.

My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning. My research interests include:
- LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
- Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)
- LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability
